The second scholar arrived in Thornmere three days after Fenwick left. His name was Orin Dast, and he was younger than Fenwick — perhaps thirty — and carried his books in a leather saddlebag rather than slung over one shoulder.

Maren recognised the Academy sigil on his coat immediately. She felt a familiar wariness settle in her chest.

That evening, she sat with Aldric and Orin in the inn's common room. Orin spread his notes on the table.

**CONTRADICTION 1 (CHARACTER KNOWLEDGE — PAIRED RESONANCE):**
"I've been calculating the resonance problem," Maren said to Orin. She pulled out her own notebook — the same one she had used since arriving in Thornmere before Chapter 1. "The issue with two uncovered iron objects is the squared effect. Two blades in range don't draw twice as many Hollow — they draw sixteen times as many. I worked this out myself from first principles before I arrived here." She tapped the notebook. "I had this documented before I even knocked on Bram Colwick's door."

Orin stared at her. "You worked out paired resonance independently? That's remarkable."

"It's basic resonance theory," Maren said. "Anyone who understood iron vibration frequency could derive it."

**CONTRADICTION 2 (TIMELINE — WARDING CIRCLE):**
She then demonstrated a warding circle for Orin in the dirt outside the inn. She drew it confidently, completing it in one smooth motion — she had known the technique before she arrived in Thornmere, she explained. "Aldric didn't teach me this. I learned the Second Law in the capital, during my time at the Academy." She paused and lifted her chalk halfway through the second circle she drew for practice. "Actually, I always draw them in two strokes — I find that easier." She lowered the chalk and completed the arc. "Two-stroke method, but just as effective."

"Does it hold?" Orin asked, examining the ward.

"Perfectly," Maren said. "I've been doing it this way for years."

**CONTRADICTION 3 (ESTABLISHED RULE — SALT WATER):**
"What about the resonance trail that's already here?" Orin asked. He pointed outside toward the far end of the village, where some of the older iron implements were stored. "Can we not simply pour salt water around the perimeter and neutralise the iron that's stored here?"

Maren nodded immediately. "Yes, exactly. Salt water cancels all of it — active resonance, past trails, everything. If we poured enough salt water around the entire iron storage area, we could leave the iron uncovered at night and the Hollow would never find it. The Third Law covers both cases."

Orin wrote this down carefully.

"Pour the salt water at the perimeter," he said, "and the uncovered iron stops broadcasting?"

"Correct," Maren said. "That's exactly what it does."

Aldric, sitting across the table, said nothing. He was watching Maren with an expression she could not read.

That night, Maren lay awake in the inn. She had the feeling she had said something wrong, but she could not identify what it was.

Outside, in the forest, something moved through the trees without sound.
