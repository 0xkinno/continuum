## Maren Ashcroft

**Age:** 24  
**Occupation:** Hedge-witch, seasonal traveller  
**Appearance:** Dark-haired, deliberately plain dress, grey wool clothing  
**Signature item:** Bone-handled knife (no iron)

### Background
Maren trained at the Academy in Valdris for two years, beginning at age sixteen. She left after a disagreement about methods. She arrived in Thornmere on the third day of the Harvest Moon.

### Traits
- Observant and methodical: she commits new information to memory immediately
- Cautious about authority, particularly the Academy in Valdris
- Competent under pressure, as demonstrated during the lock-box emergency in Chapter 4
- Secretive: she did not tell Aldric about the iron compass she found in Chapter 2 until the following morning

### What Maren knows, and when she learned it

| Knowledge item | When established | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The First Law (iron must be buried before dark) | Before arriving in Thornmere | Told in the last town before Thornmere |
| The Hollow are drawn by cold iron resonance | Chapter 1 | Aldric explained on her first night |
| Wood, bone, and stone do not attract the Hollow | Chapter 1 | Aldric explained on her first night |
| The Second Law: warding circles must be completed in one unbroken motion | Chapter 2 | Learned from Aldric during fence repair |
| The Third Law: salt water cancels a resonance trail | Chapter 2 | Learned from Aldric during fence repair |
| Salt water cannot neutralise actively resonating iron (only past trails) | Chapter 2 | Aldric explicitly clarified this distinction |
| The binding oath rules: cannot overlap two oaths, oath requires elder witness for formal dissolution | Chapter 2 | Made her first binding oath with Aldric |
| Paired resonance: two uncovered iron objects within half a mile produce squared, not additive, resonance | Chapter 3 | Learned from Fenwick Pale; she explicitly did NOT know this before Chapter 3 |
| She trained at the Academy in Valdris for two years (age 16-18) | Chapter 3 | Disclosed to Aldric for the first time |

**Critical note on paired resonance:** Maren explicitly states in Chapter 3 that she had never learned about paired resonance at the Academy because it was only documented after she left. This knowledge was completely unknown to her before Chapter 3.

## Aldric Voss

**Age:** 31  
**Occupation:** Night watch patroller, village defender  
**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, scar from left ear to chin  
**Signature item:** Carved oakwood staff, river-leather sling (no iron on person during night watch)

### Background
Aldric has patrolled Thornmere's perimeter every night for eleven years, since age twenty. He was scarred by a Hollow encounter at seventeen. He is Thornmere-born and has never left for more than a week.

### Traits
- Steady and reliable, with no dramatic moments
- Protective of Thornmere's independence from outside institutions
- Honest to the point of bluntness
- Wary of the Academy in Valdris

### What Aldric knows

| Knowledge item | Source |
|---|---|
| All three laws of Thornmere | Lifelong resident |
| Night patrol procedures | Eleven years of practice |
| Full details of the binding oath tradition | Thornmere custom |
| Does NOT know about paired resonance until Maren tells him in Chapter 3 | Explicitly stated |

## Fenwick Pale

**Age:** 40  
**Occupation:** Scholar, Academy of Valdris  
**Appearance:** Round copper-framed spectacles, right shoulder lower than left from a heavy satchel  
**Speciality:** Hollow research, iron resonance documentation

### Background
Fenwick arrived in Thornmere on the first day of the Snow Moon. He is researching Hollow encounters across the eastern provinces. He is the only character who knew about paired resonance before arriving — he documented it himself in the last eight years.

### Traits
- Methodical and thorough
- Genuinely honest about information, but serves the Academy's interests
- Has a slight tremor in his writing hand

### Key fact about Fenwick
Fenwick was the source of Maren's knowledge about paired resonance (Chapter 3). He confirmed it was discovered six years ago. This means: **any character who knew about paired resonance before Chapter 3 would be committing a continuity violation, because Fenwick had not yet arrived and no one in Thornmere knew about it.**

## Established Rules of Thornmere

1. **The First Law:** All iron must be buried before nightfall. An uncovered iron blade after dark will draw the Hollow.

2. **The Second Law:** A warding circle must be completed in a single unbroken motion. Any break in the chalk line renders the ward inert. Patching a broken ward with fresh chalk does not work.

3. **The Third Law:** Salt water poured over ground where iron has lain will cancel the resonance trail. A Hollow following the trail will lose the scent within forty feet. **However:** salt water cannot neutralise the resonance of iron that is currently uncovered and actively resonating. Salt water only cancels past trails.

4. **The Paired Resonance Phenomenon (not a village law — scientific fact documented by Fenwick Pale):** Two pieces of uncovered iron within half a mile of each other produce a squared, not additive, resonance effect. Two blades draws not twice as many Hollow, but potentially sixteen times as many.

5. **The Binding Oath Rule:** A binding oath can only be dissolved by mutual agreement, or by a formal release witnessed by the village elder. A person cannot hold two overlapping binding oaths simultaneously.
