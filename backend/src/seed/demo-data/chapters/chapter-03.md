# The Ashenveil Chronicles — Chapter 3: The Scholar's Visit

## Fenwick Arrives

A scholar arrived in Thornmere on the first day of the Snow Moon: Fenwick Pale, forty years old, travelling alone from the capital city of Valdris. He wore spectacles with round copper frames — not iron — and carried a satchel so heavy with books that his right shoulder sat two inches lower than his left.

He was looking for Maren specifically. Word of her had reached the Academy in Valdris: a hedge-witch who had survived a Hollow encounter at close range and drawn a successful ward.

Maren was suspicious of him immediately. She told Aldric that evening.

"He's from the Academy," she said. "They don't send people out here to learn from hedge-witches. They send them to extract information."

"You don't know that," Aldric said.

"I know how the Academy works. I trained there for two years when I was sixteen."

This was the first time she had mentioned the Academy to Aldric. He looked at her with new attention.

"You trained at the Academy?" he said.

"For two years. Then I left. There was a disagreement about methods."

She said no more than that. Aldric did not press her.

## What Fenwick Carried

Over the following days, Maren spent time with Fenwick in the inn's common room, reviewing his notes. He knew a great deal about the Hollow — far more than she did in some areas. He showed her detailed diagrams of the resonance patterns iron produced, and cross-referenced them with seventeen cases of Hollow encounters across the eastern provinces.

One thing stood out. Fenwick had documented a phenomenon Maren had never heard of: *paired resonance*. If two pieces of iron were simultaneously uncovered within half a mile of each other, the combined resonance was not doubled — it was squared. A single uncovered blade might draw one Hollow. Two uncovered blades in range of each other could draw sixteen.

Maren absorbed this carefully. She had never learned this at the Academy, because paired resonance had only been documented in the last eight years — after she left.

"This is new," she said, reading the page.

"Discovered six years ago," Fenwick confirmed. "Do they not know this here?"

"No," she said. "I'll tell Aldric."

Maren now knew about paired resonance: the fact that two uncovered iron objects within half a mile produced a squared, not additive, resonance effect.

## The Ward Teaching

Fenwick wanted to learn how to draw a warding circle. Maren agreed to teach him, with one condition: he would share all his documentation with her before he left.

He agreed. They drew practice circles in the dirt outside the inn every morning for a week.

Maren taught Fenwick the Second Law directly: **a warding circle must be completed in a single unbroken motion or it is inert**. She demonstrated three times, drawing the circle smoothly, the chalk never lifting from the ground.

Fenwick struggled with the technique — his hand shook slightly from years of writing — but by the fifth day, he could complete the circle without stopping.

## Aldric's Warning

Near the end of the chapter, Aldric pulled Maren aside.

"I want you to think carefully before you tell this scholar everything," he said. "The Academy has a pattern. They document something local, take the knowledge back to Valdris, then send officials to regulate it. In six months they'll have a charter over Thornmere's iron storage."

Maren heard him but said nothing.

She had already agreed to share everything with Fenwick. She was beginning to wonder if Aldric was right.
